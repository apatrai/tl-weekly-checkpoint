## Appendix 1 — RevFixr's diagnosis of us (approach summary)
RevFixr is a SaaS pricing consultancy (Amsterdam) we evaluated in Jun–Jul 2026. Their read on us — largely confirmed by our own data:
- Net revenue retention is below 100% (~96%/mo blended): the base is in net contraction — seat expansion does not outrun churn + contraction.
- Inverted retention: top-tier customers decline while bottom-tier grows; the top MRR quartile is ~69% of revenue with the worst (and falling) retention.
- No monetization-escalation levers: nothing that charges heavier or more complex users more (no triggers, caps, limits).
- Volume discounting is backwards: bigger accounts pay LESS per seat while getting more value; all discounting is squeezed onto the one seat dimension.
- Seat-only pricing "cost-stacks" against the CRM: buyers mentally add our seat price to HubSpot's seat price instead of judging our own value.
- Their thesis: selective monetization of larger accounts (upgrade ladder, add-ons, gated compliance) rather than equal per-seat treatment.

Their method: design packaging first, then validate deal-by-deal on custom-quote deals **before** touching the public pricing page (works from as few as ~5 sales conversations).

Public case they cite: [Geogap](https://revfixr.com/geogap) — segment-based plans → ARPU +65%, MRR +47%, churn −11%. Pricing pages they point to as models: Avoma, Basecamp, GoHighLevel, Fireflies.ai (seat-tier escalation).

Keep in mind: their playbook leans enterprise, while 54% of our customers have exactly 1 seat and 69% have ≤2 — treat this as input for your own judgement on questions 1–2, not as the answer.
